# Drift privacy policy

Updated September 6, 2026.

Drift is a Markdown notes app for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Your notes belong to
you. Drift does not require a Drift account, run a notes server, show ads, or
include its own analytics or tracking. Drift does not upload your notes,
filenames, or search queries to its developer. TestFlight beta diagnostics are
described below.

## Your notes and settings

Notes are ordinary Markdown files in your notebook folder. Drift reads and
writes those files to display, search, and save your notes. The app also stores
settings, folder permissions, editing positions, and copies of note content
locally for fast loading, history, and recovery. This information is not
collected by the developer.

If you use iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles storage
and syncing under its own privacy policy and your account settings. If you
share a note using your device's sharing controls, the destination you choose
receives it.

## Keeping and deleting data

Drift keeps your notes until you change or delete them. Deleting a note may
retain a copy for Undo or recovery. Local caches, history, and recovery data may
also contain earlier note content. Deleting the app does not necessarily
delete notebook folders outside the app or copies held by a file provider or
backup service. You control those files and your provider's trash and backups.

The developer cannot access, retrieve, or delete your notes remotely. For help
locating or removing local data, use the [support page](SUPPORT.md).

## Support and beta feedback

If you contact the developer or submit a bug report, the information you
choose to include is used to respond and improve Drift. GitHub issues are
public, so do not include private notes or other sensitive information.

GitHub hosts the project and these pages under its own
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
If you use a TestFlight build, Apple automatically collects crash reports and
usage information and shares them with the developer. Apple also forwards any
beta feedback you submit. This information is used to fix problems and improve
Drift, not for advertising. See Apple's
[TestFlight privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/)
for the data it collects and retains.

## Questions
